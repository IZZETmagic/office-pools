Option C’s background, scrolled. p is the same scroll progress the header already interpolates on — 0 at rest, 1 pinned. Today the crest is the only thing that reaches the pinned strip; these are the two candidates for replacing it.

1The name shrinks instead of fading

Today the crest survives and the name dies — because at the crest’s 0.53 scale the name would be four-point type. With no crest the name IS the identity, so it shrinks 19→13 and stays. Nothing disappears; two lines become one.
