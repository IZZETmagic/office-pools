=== THE ONE AND ONLY CHANGE: THE SIDEBURNS ===

The SECOND reference image is the haircut you must reproduce. Copy it EXACTLY: the same very
short buzz cut, the same hairline across the forehead, the same outline over the skull and
temples, the same slightly darker swoosh texture on top, the same two browns. It must read as
the identical haircut on the identical head.

Change ONE thing and nothing else.

⚠ THE SIDEBURNS ARE LONGER. In the reference image the hair in front of each ear stops short —
roughly level with the MIDDLE of the ear — and its bottom edge has a small notch bitten out of
it. Instead, each sideburn CONTINUES SMOOTHLY DOWNWARD past the ear, and its bottom edge ends
LEVEL WITH THE BOTTOM OF THE EAR BUMP, or a little below it.

Each sideburn is a smooth strip hugging the very edge of the face, immediately in front of the
ear. Its two long edges are smooth and roughly parallel, narrowing only gently on the way down,
and its bottom is a clean slightly rounded end. There is NO notch, NO step, NO spur, NO sharp
point and no piece cut out of it anywhere along its length.

If you are unsure how far down to take them, make them LONGER rather than shorter. Slightly too
long is fine. Stopping short is the mistake.

BOTH SIDEBURNS ARE IDENTICAL — same width, same length, ending at exactly the same height on
the left and the right.
